Playlist Sync

Point at a Spotify or Deezer playlist; anything on it that you don't own gets pulled in. Turn on auto-sync and it keeps up on a schedule.

Deezer playlists work with no setup. For Spotify, create a free app at developer.spotify.com and paste its credentials in Settings — the redirect URL to register is shown there. Audio is always downloaded via YouTube/deemix — never from the streaming platform.

Settings

General▾
Downloads & Sync▾
How often auto-synced playlists are re-checked. Default 6.
Audio sources▾

Where downloads come from when only a song name is known (synced playlists, watched artists, searches). The first source is tried first; if it fails, the next one takes over automatically. Pasting a direct URL always uses that site.

Fall back to the next source when one fails (e.g. a rate limit)
Integration (Spotify & SoundCloud)▾

Needed for Spotify playlist sync (reading) and for saving playlists to Spotify or SoundCloud (writing, one-click sign-in from the Playlists tab). Create a free Spotify app at developer.spotify.com; for SoundCloud, register an app at soundcloud.com/you/apps (Artist Pro required).

Paste this exact URL into each app's “Redirect URIs”.
